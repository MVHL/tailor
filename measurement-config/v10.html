Inspection workflow — measurement steps Mockup v10 — for review

The measurement part of one inspection workflow. Each step is a group of measurements the inspector performs; the list on the left is the order they happen in. Nothing here is specific to garment, fabric or hard goods — the step name is the admin's, and the structure below carries all three.

A column says at which level it is recorded. Four levels, one of them authored here.
Lot — once for the whole lot.garment: the order line · fabric: the dye lot
Sample — once for each unit drawn from the lot.garment: the piece · fabric: the roll
POM — once for each point of measure in the chart.only when the step has a Measurement Chart
Repeat — the same measurement taken more than once, under names you set.1st wash / 3rd wash · reading 1, 2, 3

Changed from v9: v9 made cardinality a property of the step and forced every column to share it. Two real reports break that — a moisture check records three readings per sample next to a single air temperature, and Komar's fabric report records shrinkage on one roll and rolls it up to the lot. So the level moves to the column. What keeps that from repeating v7's mistake is an explicit rule on references: a coarser value used by a finer one broadcasts, and a finer value used by a coarser one needs a named aggregate. Sampling controls are gone — sample count is derived from lot size upstream and is not knowable here.
